## English

**autoposting** is a personal automation app operated by Moonbird.Thinker. It uses the Threads API for two purposes only:

1. **Publishing** posts to Threads accounts that the operator owns and has authorized (the operator's own accounts).
2. **Curation**: once a day, searching public Threads posts from a small, fixed list of public AI-related accounts (for example AI companies and well-known researchers), creating a short Korean summary of each post, and showing it together with the author's handle and a link to the original post in a private Telegram digest for the operator and in a daily curation article on the operator's blog.

**What we collect.** For accounts the operator owns: an access token and user ID, stored only on the operator's computer to publish posts. For curated public posts: the post URL, author handle, timestamp and text, used only to generate the summary. We keep only the post URLs afterwards, to avoid showing the same post twice.

**What we do not do.** We do not republish the full text or media of other people's posts, we do not collect data about people who view or interact with posts, we do not like, reply to or follow anyone on behalf of users, and we do not sell, rent or share data with third parties. We do not use the data for advertising or profiling.

**Retention.** Tokens are kept until the operator revokes them. Curated post URLs are kept for deduplication and pruned regularly (at most the latest 500).

**Your choices.** If you are the author of a curated post and want it removed from our summaries, email pemtpemt3@gmail.com with the post URL; we remove it within 30 days. See also the [data deletion instructions](/autoposting/data-deletion/).
